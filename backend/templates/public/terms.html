Terms of use & privacy

Plain-language terms of use.

1. About {{ site_name }}

{{ site_name }} helps account holders review account information, follow activity and contact the support team. Administrators maintain account records and respond to account requests.

2. Account balances

Your dashboard displays your current account balance and a history of recorded changes. Each history entry includes the previous balance, the adjustment and the resulting balance.

3. Your account

Keep your password private. Administrators may deactivate accounts that misuse the service; your history is kept. You can ask for your account to be deactivated at any time via the Contact page.

4. Data we store

  • Your username and optional display name.
  • A one-way hash of your password (we cannot read your password).
  • Your account value and its history.
  • Messages you send us, and the date of your last sign-in.

Data is stored in a database on the server running this site and is not shared with anyone.

5. Cookies

We use a single, essential session cookie to keep you signed in. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.
