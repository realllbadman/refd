Protect your administrator account with a second step at sign-in.

{{ icon("shield") }} Two-step sign-in

After your password, you’ll enter a 6-digit code from an authenticator app (for example Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password or Bitwarden).

{{ 'On' if current_user.totp_secret else 'Off' }}

  1. In your authenticator app, add an account by entering a setup key.
  2. Use this key (time-based):

    Or open the setup link on a device with an authenticator app.

  3. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows.

To turn it off, confirm your password and a current code.

{{ icon("info") }} Lost your device?

Another administrator can turn off two-step sign-in for you from your account page under Accounts. Doing so signs you out everywhere and is recorded in the audit log.

If you are the only administrator, save the setup key somewhere safe when you turn this on. It isn’t shown again, and with it you can add your account to a new device.
